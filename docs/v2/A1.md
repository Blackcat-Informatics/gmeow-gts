# A1: Record of measurements

Non-normative. No requirement of any part refers to this annex; a constant of the canonical form and a rule that rests on a
figure name the entry that holds the figure, and nothing more. An entry is never frozen.

## 0. How to read an entry

Each entry has one stable identifier and the same five fields.

- QUESTION: what the measurement was asked.
- METHOD: what was run, on what kind of input, with which parameters and how many times. An input is described by its kind and its
  size only.
- FIGURES: every figure with its unit and the condition under which it was taken. A figure measures either the FORMAT (bytes and
  counts, valid for any implementation) or an IMPLEMENTATION (time or memory of a named library or engine); the entry says which.
- STATUS, one of two values:
  - `closed`: the figures answer the question as recorded; what the run did not measure is listed in the entry under
  "not measured".
  - `open`: owed. The entry holds the question, the method and a decision rule fixed in advance, and no figure.
- RESTS ON: the constant or rule, by part and section title, that cites the entry. An entry that no part cites says
  "background" and names the section that its figures bear on.

REQUIREMENT LANGUAGE. This document states no requirement and uses no key word of BCP 14 [RFC2119] [RFC8174].

## 1. Conditions common to the entries

THE REFERENCE BUNDLE is one first-generation file used as the real input of the size measurements. Its size class: 282,425,878
bytes stored in 20 frames (281,818,286 stored payload bytes; 3,008,808,927 decoded bytes). Its shape: a snapshot of 4,199,087
distinct terms (all used) and 16,686,716 quads without duplicate, 1,443 `reifies` rows and 2,059 `annot` rows; 186,485 blank
nodes; 19 blobs of 1,714,357,739 bytes in all, two of them 92 per cent of the blob bytes; 7 literals of 64 KiB or more, one of
about 328 MB; 18 of the 20 frames compressed with a first-generation in-band dictionary. It holds no commit frame and no
signature. Nothing else about it is recorded here.

The host: one `x86-64` machine (32 hardware threads, `AVX-512`, 124 GiB of memory), shared with other work during every run (load
average about 58). Unless an entry says otherwise a timing was run at least five times and is given as the minimum and the
median, the minimum being the better estimate of cost. Retired user instructions come from `perf stat -e instructions:u`.

Tools: Python 3.13 with `blake3` 1.0.9, `zstandard` 0.25.0 (`libzstd` 1.5.7) and `cryptography` 50.0.1 (OpenSSL 4.0.2); `gcc`
16.2.1 with the host's `libzstd` 1.5.7 and `libblake3`; Rust (cargo 1.102, nightly), Go 1.27.4 and Node 27, all built offline.
No Java runtime and no Smalltalk image were present: those engines were not run in any entry. The reference engine of this
generation is pure Python and holds a file in memory; bytes and counts made with it, or with a native tool checked byte for byte
against it, are measurements of the format; its times and memory are its own.

Where the engine could not carry the reference bundle, a C tool that writes the same bytes made the frames and the packs; the
checks of each tool against the text and against the engine are stated below. Compression in the size entries is `libzstd`
1.5.7, one-shot, one zstd frame for each frame, level `12`, window log `26`, content size recorded, checksum clear, unless the
entry says otherwise. Sizes are payload bytes after compression and exclude envelopes unless
stated.

The entries name codings by the words run-length, delta, conditional delta and plain. The codings of the format are those of
`P3` "3.1 Columns and their fixed codings"; the others are alternatives measured against them.

THE BLOB ENTRIES. In FE6-2, FE6-7, U-B, FE6-8, FE6-9, FE6-10 and the value region of FE5-5 the gear table is the first 2,048 bytes
of the BLAKE3 key-derivation output with the context `gts2 gear table` and empty key material, read as 256 big-endian 64-bit words
(BLAKE3-256 `47436b36d649182d7c4f58b1454e64fd1aef2e5a564bd1a21f913fbbb45d6c4a`); the constants are `min` 16384, `b` 16, `max`
262144, `n` 2, `R` = `W` = 64 unless an entry says otherwise. Hashes are `H_ctx(unit, ...)`. Members, pack directories and recipe
bodies are the encodings of `P4` sections 3, 4 and 7, written by one C tool; every pack is one zstd frame by `libzstd` 1.5.7
`ZSTD_compress2` with level `12` and window log `26` as the only parameters, unless an entry says otherwise. THE TOOL OF THOSE
ENTRIES was checked in three ways. (1) Its chunk lengths equal those of the chunker of OW-3 (itself proved equal to the engine's
chunker) on the 19 blobs: 15,894 chunks, 19 of 19 blobs equal. (2) Its decoded pack payloads equal, byte for byte, those of the
engine's `gts2ref.blobs.regions` and `pack_payload` (read only) on 13 of the blobs (13,883,883 bytes, all those below 4 MiB) for
the blob region and on 571 of the literals (6,735,283 bytes, each below 400 KB) for the value region, at pack sizes 1 MiB and 16
MiB: 4 of 4 comparisons equal, 14 + 1 + 7 + 1 packs. (3) The frames of the Python `zstandard` binding at level `12` and window log
`26` equal the C tool's frames on three packs.

No entry measures a typed value.

THE RDF FRAMES OF THE SIZE ENTRIES. In FE5-2, FE5-3, FE5-4, FE5-5, FE5-13 and OW-1 the `terms` and `quads` frames are the canonical
frames of the reference bundle's walked snapshot (4,199,087 distinct terms, 16,686,716 quads without duplicate; the 1,443
`reifies` rows and 2,059 `annot` rows are not written) under the layout of `P3` 2.2, 2.3, 2.5, 3.1 and 11.1: kind order `iri`,
`literal`, `literal_ref`, `blank`, `triple` (the bundle holds no typed value, so no `literal_value` kind); a literal is one kind
with a column `forms`, `datatypes` for the typed terms only and `language_indexes` for the language terms only; the order within
`literal` is (lexical form, form, datatype or language); IRIs, blank labels `E(position)` and triple terms as `P3` 11.1; a literal
of 1,048,576 bytes or more is a `literal_ref` (3 terms, 346,829,167 bytes of text) with the grammar `[5, value_digests, lengths,
forms, datatypes, language_indexes]`, its octets the UTF-8 bytes of its lexical form, its digest `H_ctx(unit, octets)` (`P1` 4.1).
The cut is `Bt` 4,194,304 with the addend `8`, `Ng` 64 restarting at the first term of each run, `Nr` 1,048,576. Each frame is
compressed alone with `libzstd` 1.5.7, `ZSTD_compress2`, level `12` and window log `26` the only parameters set (content size
recorded, checksum clear); stored bytes are the bytes of that zstd frame and exclude envelopes and the 16-byte stored check. A
frame is `stored_compressed` when its decoded length is at least 64 and the zstd frame plus 16 is smaller; none of the frames
below fell under that rule. The columns are typed arrays unless an entry says otherwise. `quads` frames hold the graph as
`graph_runs`. One C tool writes the frames, with the layout and the constants as parameters. All figures are measurements of the
FORMAT (bytes and counts), on one dataset, one run each (bytes are deterministic).

THE TOOL OF THOSE ENTRIES was checked in four ways. (1) The payload bytes of `P3` 12.1 (57 bytes) and 12.2 (28 bytes) and of the
`iri`, `literal` and `literal_ref` blocks of the second payload of 12.1 (100 bytes without its `literal_value` block, with the
digest `H_ctx(unit, 61 62 63)` computed separately) are reproduced exactly. (2) An independent validator (Python, no code shared
with the tool) sorted the walk under `P3` 11.1 itself and got the order of the tool for all 4,199,087 terms; decoded all 138
`terms` payloads under the grammar of 2.2 (block shapes, list lengths, front coding with a restart at every 64th term and the
longest common prefix, the language table in order of first use, labels `E(position)`, digests `H_ctx(unit, utf8)`), found the
decoded term of every id equal to the walk term at that place of the order, recomputed the cut of `P7` 4.2 and found it equal;
decoded all 16 `quads` frames to rows that are strictly ascending, equal to the sorted rows, and whose multiset equals the raw
rows mapped through the ids. (3) Every zstd frame was decompressed inside the tool and compared with its payload. (4) The window
log makes no difference to the frames of the format: the same run at window log 27 gives the same stored bytes (44,060,148 and
9,579,465), because no frame of the format exceeds 2^26 decoded bytes. It matters only to two rows that run at 26 here and have
a frame above 2^26: at window log 27 the `none` row of FE5-5 stores 83,618,631 bytes (0.01 per cent less) and the count `1048576`
row of FE5-3 35,936,416 (0.44 per cent less).

THE NOISE FLOOR OF THE ENCODER. A change of 4 bytes in the length of one `terms` payload changed the stored size of that frame by
15,211 bytes of 541,229 (the two payloads differ in 125 bytes of 1,549,428 and the 404,451 bytes that follow are equal), so
`terms` totals of one layout vary by about 0.03 per cent with details of the payload that are not the subject of any comparison
here. Differences below 0.05 per cent are not findings.

BLANK LABELS. The labels are `E(position)` with the position taken from the source order of the walk, not from the labelling `L1`
of this fold (the labelling of the bundle was not computed). This changes the row order among the rows that name blank nodes only.
